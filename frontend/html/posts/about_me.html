About me

I’m engineer specializing in backend development.

For as long as I can remember, I’ve been fascinated by technology and engineering. As a kid, I loved taking my toys apart to see how they were built and designed, and I found it incredibly satisfying to understand the logic behind their construction. At the time, I was simply following my curiosity, unaware that I was actually doing pure reverse engineering, a powerful learning tool that taught me not only how to disassemble my toys, but also how to put them back together.

Today, code is my primary tool, but my interest in engineering goes well beyond programming. I also enjoy designing functional objects and 3D-printing them.

Whenever I feel I need a break from the digital world, I enjoy taking analog (film) photos. I love the idea of capturing life using only chemistry, optics, and mechanics.
