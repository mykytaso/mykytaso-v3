About me

I’m engineer specializing in backend development.

For as long as I can remember, I’ve been passionate about tech and engineering. As a kid, I loved disassembling my toys and figuring out how they were built and designed, and I found it incredibly satisfying to understand the decisions behind their design. Back then, I was simply following my curiosity and didn’t realize I was actually doing pure reverse engineering, a powerful learning tool that taught me not only how to take my toys apart, but also how to put them back together.

These days, code is my primary tool, but my love of engineering goes far beyond programming. I also enjoy designing functional objects and 3D-printing them.

Whenever I feel I need a break from the digital world, I enjoy taking analog (film) photos. I love the idea of capturing life using only chemistry, optics, and mechanics.
